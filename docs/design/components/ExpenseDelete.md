# ExpenseDelete

Confirming a delete.

Delete Expense opens a second sheet that must be answered. It repeats the amount and what it was for, says the delete cannot be undone, and offers "Delete Expense" in `danger` and "Keep It". After a delete the row leaves the list and the month's total drops by its amount.
