# Attendance

The Attendance tab as the front desk sees it, at phone width and full scroll length.

A tab screen for one branch, one day at a time. Top to bottom: the title with the day and branch under it, the DayStrip (the last seven days, today selected), a Members or Staff switch (SegmentedControl, Members first), then on the Members side the Check In Member action, two figures (check-ins today, check-ins since the start of this hour), check-ins by hour, who is booked and not here yet, then the day's check-ins with the newest first. The title scrolls away; the DayStrip stays pinned.

You provide each day's total, its check-ins by hour and its visits, plus the members to search. The script in `preview.html` switches days, filters today's list, checks members in, removes a wrong check-in and keeps every figure in step. Replace its demo data with your own query.

- Members or Staff: the Staff side lists the trainers and receptionists of this branch for the chosen day. See AttendanceStaff. The DayStrip drives both sides.
- One day at a time. The DayStrip swaps the figures, the chart and the list together. For anything longer than a week, use Reports.
- Today can be changed; earlier days are a record. Check In Member and Remove Check-In show for today only.
- Check In Member opens the same search and the same CheckInSheet as Receptionist Home. A member who has already checked in today shows "Checked in 8:38 AM" in place of the button, so nobody is checked in twice in a day. An expired membership is blocked, with Renew and Collect Payment.
- What the gym records: a check-in is a member (or staff member) and a time, nothing else. No check-out, no time spent inside, no "in the gym now", no method. Every figure here is a count of check-ins.
- The chips filter by the branch's sessions (its opening hours, set on Add or Edit Branch): All, then one chip per session. They always add up to All. See AttendanceByTime.
- Tapping a row opens the VisitSheet. Rows carry no buttons.
- The chart follows the dashboard chart rules: one series in `ink`, the current hour in `volt`, hours still to come left empty and not tappable.
- No rupee amounts appear here. A payment due shows on the member's status badge and on their profile.
- An admin opens the same screen from Mark Attendance. Trainers do not get this screen.
- Related states: AttendanceStaff, AttendanceStaffShift, AttendanceByTime, AttendancePastDay, AttendanceVisit, AttendanceRemove, AttendanceSearch, AttendanceCheckedIn, AttendanceEmpty, AttendanceLoading, AttendanceWide.
