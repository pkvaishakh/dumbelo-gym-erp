# MembersList

The Members list as the front desk and admins see it, at phone width and full scroll length.

A list screen: the front desk's Members tab, and for admins the first row of More. Top to bottom: the title with the branch under it, the search field, the status FilterChips with their counts, a line saying how many are shown and in what order, then one MemberRow per member in a single card, and Load More. The title scrolls away; the search field and the chips stay pinned.

You provide the members, the totals per status and the profile target. The script in `preview.html` filters as you type, applies the chip and the Sort and filter sheet, and swaps in the EmptyState when nothing matches. Replace its demo data with your own query.

- Tapping a row opens that member's profile. Rows carry no buttons: check-in, renewal and payment live on the profile and on Receptionist Home.
- Search here narrows the list in place. It matches the start of a name, a phone number or a member ID, the same rule as the Receptionist Home search.
- "Active" counts every membership that can check in, so it includes Expiring and Payment due. All is Active plus Expired plus Frozen.
- The default order is by name. No revenue and no totals in rupees appear here; a row shows only that member's own balance.
- Trainers get their own list of assigned members, not this one.
- Related states: MembersListFiltered, MembersListSearch, MembersListEmpty, MembersListSort, MembersListLoading, MembersListWide.
