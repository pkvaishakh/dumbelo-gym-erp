# Reports

The Admin's Reports tab for the last 7 days, at phone width and full scroll length.

A tab screen for owners and managers. One period control sits under the title and stays pinned: 7 days, 30 days, 3 months. Everything below answers to it. Top to bottom: four summary figures (revenue, new members, check-ins, renewals), then Revenue with its chart and a BarList by plan, Money in and out with expenses by category, Members with new members and a short list of renewed, lapsed and net change, Attendance with check-ins by day and the busiest hours, and Trainers by sessions taken.

You provide the figures for each period. The script in `preview.html` swaps the whole report when the period changes, shows a bar's or point's value on tap, hover or focus, and opens the branch sheet.

- One period and one branch for the whole screen. No chart has its own filter.
- Every summary figure carries its change against the period before, in words: "+9% vs previous 7 days".
- Seven days is drawn as bars with today in `volt`. Longer periods are a line. Each chart has one series.
- Revenue and new members are the same series Admin Home shows, so the totals agree.
- "Left over" is revenue minus expenses for the period. It is not called profit: it leaves out anything not entered as an expense.
- "View expenses" beside Money in and out opens Expenses.
- Only admins see Reports.
- Related: Reports30Days, Reports3Months, ReportsWide.
