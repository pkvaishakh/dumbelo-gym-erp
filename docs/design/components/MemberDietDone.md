# MemberDietDone

The Diet tab once every meal is ticked.

The ring is full, each macro bar is full, the line under the summary reads "You've met today's target. Well eaten." and no meal carries the "Next" tag.

- Do not celebrate beyond that line. Going over target is not flagged in `danger`; the trainer follows it up.
