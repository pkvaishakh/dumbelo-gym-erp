# MemberProgressLogWeight

The Progress tab with the Log weight sheet open.

One large field in the AmountField style with "kg" after the number, a hint on when to weigh, two buttons that move the value by 0.1 kg, and Save Weight. The field opens with the last entry in it, so most days it takes a nudge and a save.

- A value outside 30 to 250 kg shows "Enter your weight in kg, for example 71.2" under the field and saves nothing.
- Saving updates the figure, the goal card and the last point of the chart, then closes the sheet.
