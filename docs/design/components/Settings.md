# Settings

The gym's own rules, for owners and managers.

A detail screen behind More. Four sections: Gym (the name members and staff see), Check-in (grace period after a plan ends, and whether members with a payment due or a frozen membership may check in), Reminders to members (the channels, then one Switch per reminder) and Payments (the methods Collect Payment offers, GST, automatic receipts).

The script in `preview.html` opens each choice in a sheet and writes the new value back into its row.

- Everything applies at once and at every branch. A Switch has no Save button; a choice applies the moment it is tapped.
- A row with a value and a chevron opens a sheet: a list to pick one (grace period, GST) or chips to pick several (channels, payment methods). At least one must stay picked.
- Each option says what it means in practice ("They can check in for 3 days after it ends", "₹1,500 plus ₹270 GST").
- Related: SettingsGracePeriod, SettingsReminderChannels, SettingsWide.
