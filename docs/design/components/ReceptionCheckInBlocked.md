# ReceptionCheckInBlocked

The sheet shown when check-in is refused because the membership has expired.

Same layout as the confirmation, with a `danger-soft` mark, "Check-in blocked", one sentence saying why and what fixes it, and the member's card showing the plan and the date it expired. The two buttons are the two ways forward: "Renew" (primary) and "Collect Payment" (secondary). The close button, the scrim or Escape dismisses it without doing either. See CheckInSheet.
