# AttendanceStaff

The Staff side of Attendance: the trainers and receptionists of this branch, for the day chosen in the DayStrip.

Top to bottom: two figures, one for trainers and one for receptionists, then the Staff list with its filter chips. Today each figure reads "2 of 3, Trainers checked in": checked in out of those working today, with a line saying who is due later and who has the day off. On an earlier day it reads "3 of 4, Trainers checked in", out of everyone in that role. The two figures always count separately and do not change with the filter chips.

You provide the people (name, role, what they do or their shift, usual hours, day off) and each day's check-ins. The script in `preview.html` switches days and filters, opens a person's day, and checks staff in. Replace its demo data with your own query.

- Everyone who works at the branch is listed every day, including people on their day off, so the list is a register, not only a log.
- Two rows of filter chips: All, Trainers, Receptionists; then Any session and one chip per branch session, each with how many checked in during it. Both filters apply together.
- Each row: photo, name, role and speciality or shift; on the right the check-in time and one status: the session they checked in for ("Morning"), Due 2:00 PM, or Day off. A check-in during a break counts for the next session. A staff check-in, like a member's, is only a name and a time: no check-out and no hours worked. Before check-in the time reads "Not in" or "Off" in `ink-muted`.
- Order: checked in first, earliest first, then due later, then day off.
- Tapping a row opens AttendanceStaffShift. Rows carry no buttons.
- Today can be changed; earlier days are a record.
- The front desk and the admin both see this side. Trainers do not get this screen.
