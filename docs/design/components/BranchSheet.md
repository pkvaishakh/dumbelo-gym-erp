# BranchSheet

One branch, opened from its row.

A sheet with the branch name as its title and a DetailList: address, phone, opening hours (one session per line, "Morning, 5 to 10 AM", then the days), active members, staff (trainers and receptionists, counted from the Staff list), and the date it opened. Edit Branch opens EditBranch. View Staff opens the Staff list at that branch's group; it is hidden for a branch with no staff yet.
