# AdminMore

The Admin's More tab: everything that is not one of the four tabs.

A list screen with two cards. Your gym: Members, Trainers, Receptionists, Tasks, Expenses, Branches, Plans and Pricing, each with its count or total on the right. Account: Account Details, Settings, Sign Out. Each row is an icon chip, a label, an optional value and a chevron.

- Members opens MembersList with More still marked current in the bottom bar; for the front desk, Members stays a tab.
- Trainers and Receptionists both open StaffList, with its Trainers or Receptionists chip already chosen; the All chip is still there. Each value counts that role across every branch ("11 trainers").
- Tasks opens Tasks, with how many are open on the right.
- Trainers, Receptionists, Tasks and Expenses are admin-only. The front desk's and trainers' More tabs do not list them (see ReceptionMore, TrainerMore, MemberMore).
- Branches opens Branches, Plans and Pricing opens PlansPricing, and Settings opens Settings. Account Details opens AccountDetails; every role has that row.
- The value on the right is a fact, not a badge: "11 trainers", "₹2,84,500 this month".
