# AdminHome

The Admin Home screen at phone width, shown at its full scroll length: what an owner or manager opens many times a day.

It answers three questions in order. How is today going: the overview cards. What needs my attention: the Needs attention card. What do I do next: quick actions, the schedule and the create button. Top to bottom: AppHeader, five StatCards (today’s revenue, active members, new members today, expired, outstanding dues), four ActionTiles, AttentionList, CheckinsByShift, ScheduleList, PerformanceChart, ActivityList, BottomNav.

You provide the data, the navigation targets and the branch list. The script in `preview.html` opens and closes the two sheets, condenses the header on scroll, switches the chart's measure and period, and shows chart tooltips; keep that behaviour and replace the demo data.

- Keep the section order. Do not add a section above the overview.
- A count shown in two places must match: outstanding dues and the Pending payments row; Expired and the Expired chip on Members; today's revenue and the chart's last bar; staff check-ins and the Today line on each staff profile.
- Tapping Expired opens Members filtered to Expired; Outstanding dues opens Members filtered to Payment due. In Check-ins today, the Members row opens Attendance and the staff rows open the Staff side of Attendance, filtered to that role.
- Show the branch chip only to admins with more than one branch; otherwise show the branch name as plain `body-sm` text.
- The Attendance tab opens Attendance. The Reports tab opens Reports. The More tab opens AdminMore, which leads to MembersList, StaffList and Expenses.
- Related states: AdminHomeLoading, AdminHomeCreateSheet, AdminHomeBranchSheet. Wider screens: AdminHomeWide.
