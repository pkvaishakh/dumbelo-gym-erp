# StaffMoveBranch

Moving a person to another branch, from their Staff Profile.

Move Branch opens a radio sheet titled "Move Arjun Rao" listing the branches, with the `volt` check on the current one. Choosing another moves the person at once: the branch under their name and in Work changes, and on Staff their row leaves one branch's card and joins the other's.

- A trainer's members stay with the trainer unless the admin reassigns them.
