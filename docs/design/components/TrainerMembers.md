# TrainerMembers

The trainer's Members tab: only the members assigned to them.

A list screen titled My Members. Search by name, and chips for All, Personal training and Needs attention, with counts. Each row: name, the next session (or, when something needs doing, what it is in `attention`), the member's goal, and how much of their pack is used. Rows open the trainer's view of the Member Profile.

- Needs attention matches Trainer Home: measurements due, a membership about to end, missed sessions, no check-in for a week or more.
- No phone numbers, payments or membership prices here.
- Related: TrainerMembersAttention, TrainerMembersSearch, MembersList (the front desk's list of everyone).
