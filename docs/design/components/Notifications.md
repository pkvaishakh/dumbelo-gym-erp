# Notifications

What the bell on a home screen opens. Shown for an admin.

A detail screen: back arrow, the title with the unread count under it, then Today and Earlier. Each notification is one row: an icon, one sentence with the person or figure in bold, and when. Unread rows are in semibold with a `volt` dot. A row that leads somewhere has a chevron; tapping it marks it read and goes there. Mark All as Read clears every dot. The role's bottom navigation stays, with Home current.

The script in `preview.html` marks rows read, updates the count, and sends `rl:notice-go` and `rl:notices-changed` (for the bell's badge).

- One sentence per notification, about one thing, written to the reader: "Farhan Ali checked in with ₹1,500 due. Collect it before he leaves."
- Each role gets only what it can act on. The admin hears about payments, new members, staff requests and reports; the front desk about who is due, expiring or expected; a trainer about their own members; a member about their own plan.
- The badge on the bell equals the unread count here.
- Related: NotificationsReceptionist, NotificationsTrainer, NotificationsMember, NotificationsAllRead.
